# Product Spec: AI/SaaS PSEO Decision Platform

## Core positioning

A practical AI software and workflow guide for professionals, small businesses, freelancers, creators, agencies, and teams in English-speaking markets.

## What the site should help users do

- Choose an AI/SaaS tool.
- Compare two tools.
- Find alternatives to a tool.
- Build an AI workflow for a business task.
- Find prompts/templates by profession or task.
- Estimate SaaS costs.
- Discover tools by country, profession, category, and budget.

## Primary audiences

1. Small business owners
2. Freelancers
3. Consultants
4. Marketing agencies
5. Sales teams
6. Recruiters
7. Real estate agents / estate agents
8. Accountants / bookkeepers
9. Ecommerce sellers
10. Developers

## Page families

- Tool detail pages
- Best tools for profession pages
- Best tools by country pages
- Tool alternatives pages
- Tool-vs-tool comparisons
- Workflow pages
- Prompt/template pages
- Free tool pages
- Editorial/static trust pages

## MVP success criteria

- 100–250 useful pages can be generated from structured data.
- Each generated page has unique value, not just swapped keywords.
- Search engines can crawl the site cleanly.
- The site has clear affiliate and editorial disclosures.
- The codebase can expand to thousands of pages after real data appears.

## Expansion model

Start with one cluster. Example:

Best AI tools for recruiters
→ ChatGPT prompts for recruiters
→ AI candidate screening workflow
→ Best recruitment CRM with AI
→ AI tools for recruiters UK
→ Claude vs ChatGPT for recruiters
→ Free recruiter outreach generator

Then repeat for other professions, countries, and tasks.
